# Contributing to ÆtherDesk

ÆtherDesk is licensed under GPL-3.0-only; see [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Stephen de la Vega is the sole copyright holder and also distributes the app
through the Mac App Store. Contributions are accepted only with the
contributor’s written agreement that Stephen de la Vega may also distribute
their contribution under the Mac App Store’s terms. Include that agreement
with your pull request.

File issues in this repository’s issue tracker. Include your macOS and app
versions, steps to reproduce, expected and actual behaviour, and relevant
logs with personal information removed.

Before submitting a pull request, run `scripts/test-gate.sh --with-js` on
macOS with Xcode and Node.js installed. This runs the Swift and JavaScript
tests. Keep changes focused and describe how you verified them.
