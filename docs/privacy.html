ÆtherDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ÆtherDesk is a macOS application that displays live wallpapers on your desktop. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains what data ÆtherDesk does and does not collect.

No Data Collected

ÆtherDesk does not collect any personal information, and we operate no servers that could receive it. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No analytics or usage telemetry is gathered.
  • No advertising identifiers are used.
  • No crash reports are sent to any server.

Location

If you use the bundled Weather Aether wallpaper, ÆtherDesk may request access to your location. Location is used only to show local weather and is never sent to us. To do that, the wallpaper sends your location directly from your Mac to the following services, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Apple WeatherKit: your coordinates, to fetch the forecast.
  • Apple geocoding: your coordinates, to determine the forecast location's time zone.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): your coordinates, to fetch the forecast when WeatherKit is unavailable or you choose Open-Meteo as the weather source; and the place name you type, if you set a location manually.
  • BigDataCloud (bigdatacloud.com): your coordinates, to look up the name of your city.
  • ipapi (ipapi.co): if location access is denied or unavailable, the wallpaper asks this service for an approximate location based on your IP address.

Setting a manual location in the wallpaper's properties avoids sending device coordinates. Your last known location and forecast are cached locally on your Mac so the wallpaper can display immediately at launch.

Network Access

Wallpapers may make outbound network requests to fetch live data (such as weather). As with any internet request, the services contacted can see your IP address. Wallpapers you import from third parties may contact services not listed here; ÆtherDesk restricts them from reaching your local network by default, but cannot control what remote services they use. ÆtherDesk does not operate any backend servers.

Software Updates (OSS Build)

The open-source build uses Sparkle to check for updates at sdelavega.github.io. An update check sends only the app version and macOS version, nothing else. System profiling is disabled. As with any internet request, the host can see your IP address. You can disable automatic update checks in Preferences › General. The App Store build receives updates through Apple and does not include Sparkle.

Local Storage

ÆtherDesk stores your wallpaper preferences and settings locally on your Mac using macOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Content

Users may import wallpaper bundles created by third parties. ÆtherDesk does not inspect, upload, or transmit the contents of imported wallpapers.

Children's Privacy

ÆtherDesk does not collect any data from any user, including children.
