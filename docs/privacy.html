ÆtherDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

ÆtherDesk is a macOS application that displays live wallpapers on your desktop. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains what data ÆtherDesk does and does not collect.

No Data Collected

ÆtherDesk does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No analytics or usage telemetry is gathered.
  • No advertising identifiers are used.
  • No crash reports are sent to any server.

Location

If you use a weather-based wallpaper, ÆtherDesk may request access to your location. This is used solely to fetch local weather conditions via Apple's WeatherKit framework. Your location is sent directly to Apple's servers under Apple's own privacy policy and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

Network Access

Wallpapers may make outbound network requests to fetch live data (such as weather). These requests are initiated by the wallpaper content and do not include any personal information. ÆtherDesk does not operate any backend servers.

Local Storage

ÆtherDesk stores your wallpaper preferences and settings locally on your Mac using macOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Content

Users may import wallpaper bundles created by third parties. ÆtherDesk does not inspect, upload, or transmit the contents of imported wallpapers.

Children's Privacy

ÆtherDesk does not collect any data from any user, including children.
